PARC FERMÉ

The app for Formula 1 fans — schedule, prediction game and fan verdicts for every race weekend.

Unofficial fan app · for Android

What this app is for

PARC FERMÉ takes you through the race weekend. The app shows every session of a Grand Prix with start times and the weather, lets you predict the race result and puts the contentious incidents of a race to a vote: you help decide whether the stewards got it right. There are also quizzes, polls and a shared chat during the race.

Features

Race calendar & sessions

Every session of a weekend — free practice, qualifying, sprint and race — with the start time in your time zone and a weather forecast.

Prediction game

Predict the outcome before the race starts and collect points. A leaderboard shows who is doing best across the season.

Fan verdicts on contentious incidents

Disputed situations are collected for every race. You vote on whether there should have been a penalty and see how the community decided — alongside the official ruling.

Watch-along & community

A shared chat during the race, plus quizzes and polls between race weekends.

Signing in

You need an account for the prediction game, votes and chat. You can sign in with your Google account — only your e-mail address and your name are taken over in order to create your profile. No other data from your Google account is accessed.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under “Profile”.
