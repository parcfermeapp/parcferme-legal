PARC FERMÉ uses exclusively technically necessary storage. There is nothing to configure, as we use no tracking, no analytics and no advertising cookies.

Necessary (always active)

Sign-in and session: keeps you logged in.

Language: remembers whether you use the app in German or English.

Profile cache: keeps your display name, favourite driver and team and your notification settings at hand so the app starts without delay. The same details are also held in your account because they are meant to apply on all your devices.

Legal texts: keeps a copy of these texts so they remain readable without a connection. The entry expires by itself after three days so no outdated version is shown.

Start screen: remembers the name, circuit and lap count of the next race so the start screen can show them immediately instead of waiting for the server. The entry expires by itself once the race is over.

Read state: remembers which headlines and inbox entries you have already opened. The inbox entry is keyed per account so that read states do not mix after switching accounts.

Notice acknowledgement: remembers that you have seen the privacy notice.

These entries reside solely on your device and are not transmitted to us. No analytics and no advertising evaluation take place. If you delete your account immediately, they are removed with it. Details in the Privacy Policy.
