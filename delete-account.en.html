Your account

Delete account

You can delete your account yourself at any time — directly in the app, or by e-mail if the app is no longer installed.

1 · In the app

  1. Open PARC FERMÉ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm the security prompt.

Your account is marked for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days. If you sign in again within that period, it is kept. To delete it right away, tap Delete now in the notice at the top of the app.

2 · By e-mail

Write to us from the e-mail address your account was created with and state your display name in the app. We carry out the deletion within 30 days.

parcferme.app@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • your user account including e-mail address and display name
  • your profile picture
  • your predictions, votes and your quiz, Start Lights, Race Word and Race Bingo results
  • your scores and leaderboard entries
  • your inbox messages and the feedback you sent
  • your device’s push identifier (notifications stop)

What remains

Posts in the public chat and in comments remain but are separated from your account: they then appear without a name and without any link to you, so that conversations stay understandable for others. If you want individual posts removed as well, say so in the same e-mail.
