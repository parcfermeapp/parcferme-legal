Delete account

PARC FERMÉ — unofficial fan app for Formula 1 fans

You can delete your account yourself at any time. There are two ways: directly in the app, or by e-mail if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open PARC FERMÉ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Confirm the security prompt.

Your account is immediately marked for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days. Within this period you can undo the process simply by signing in again.

Option 2 — By e-mail

Write to us from the e-mail address your account was created with. Please include your display name in the app so that the account can be identified clearly. The deletion is carried out within 30 days.

parcferme.app@gmail.com

Which data is deleted

With the final deletion, the following is permanently removed:

  • your user account including e-mail address and display name
  • your profile picture
  • your predictions, votes, quiz and Rennwort results
  • your scores and leaderboard entries
  • your inbox messages and the feedback you sent
  • your device’s push token (notifications stop)

What remains

Posts you wrote in the shared chat or in comments remain, but are separated from your account: afterwards they appear without a name and without any link to you. This is necessary so that conversations stay understandable for other users.

If you would also like individual posts removed, please mention this in the same e-mail.

About the 30-day period: It protects against accidental deletion. If you sign in again during this time, your account is kept in full. After it expires, the deletion is final and cannot be undone.

Detailed information on which data we process and for what purpose can be found in the Privacy Policy.
